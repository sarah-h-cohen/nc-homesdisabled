Welcome to S&C's Quality, Certification and Oversight Reports (QCOR)

What's New on QCOR?

New Features

06/05/2026 - All hospice reports have been updated with iQIES data and now reflect the latest available information.

2567 Statement of Deficiency Text Reporting - Statement of Deficiency Text reports are now available for certain provider types. Use the available filters to generate a table of citations with associated providers and surveys. In the "Statement of Deficiencies Report" column, you can view deficiency texts in either PDF or web page format.

05/04/26 - Rural Health Clinic (RHC) 2567s are now available. All RHC report data is also downloadable as an extract.

04/06/26 - Critical Access Hospital (CAH) and Transplant Hospital 2567s are now available. All CAH and Transplant data are also downloadable as extracts.

03/18/26 - Hospital 2567s are now available. All Hospital report data is also downloadable as an extract.

02/23/26 - Dialysis Facility (ESRD) 2567s are now available. All ESRD data is also downloadable as an extract.

01/15/26 - CLIA 2567s are now available. All CLIA report data is also downloadable as an extract.

Hospice 2567s are now available. The Hospice Survey Reports page contains a downloadable extract of the hospice 2567 data.

06/27/25 - Organ Procurement Organization 2567s are now available. All OPO report data is also downloadable as an extract.

01/15/25 - Emergency Medical Treatment & Labor Act (EMTALA) 2567s are now available. All EMTALA report data is also downloadable as an extract.

01/21/2026 - Several nursing home reports now utilize iQIES as their primary data source. iQIES, a cloud-based platform, is designed to replace the QIES system. The specific nursing home reports are listed below.

04/28/2025 - The Resources page has been improved with a redesign. As part of the changes, the Approved Transplant Program List has been moved to the Hospitals section under Provider Reports in the side navigation bar.

03/28/2025 - CMS no longer requires a warning banner when entering the QCOR website. QCOR URLs can now be directly linked to.



Important System Migration Notice
QCOR uses survey and certification data from two databases: QIES and iQIES. Several provider types have recently migrated to the cloud-based iQIES platform. As a result, provider, survey, and deficiency reports for some provider types do not contain up-to-date information. CMS is currently working to update QCOR using iQIES as the primary data source.
